Background is a dark red (#6b0000) to near-black charcoal (#161212, deliberately not pure #000000) gradient, red confined to the top-left corner with a wide soft blend into the gray rather than a hard edge. Gray instead of true black so the icon doesn't melt into the taskbar/title bar, which are themselves near-black. Glyph is a deepened cyan (#00a8c0) rather than the brighter #00d8f0 used elsewhere in the app, so it doesn't glare at small sizes. Same badge shape and pulse-line path as before — only the fill and stroke colors changed.
